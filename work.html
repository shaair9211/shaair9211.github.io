Traffic Engineering / Work

Selected Projects

During my time as a traffic engineer, our team used to deliver more than 400 projects a year across New South Wales and I had a hand in a good number of them, across a wide range of work, including but not limited to:

  • Traffic and parking impact studies
  • Traffic modelling
  • Swept Path Assessments
  • Compliance Reviews
  • Mechanical car stacker, car lift, and car turntable design
  • Access design
  • Construction Certification and Occupation Certification
  • Signage and line marking design
  • Car parking and loading dock facilities design
  • Traffic control plans / traffic guidance schemes
  • Construction traffic management plans
  • Green travel plans
  • Transport access guides
  • Car park management plans
  • Loading dock management plans

The picks below is just a small sample pulled from that longer list.

Note: Every project below was delivered as part of the traffic engineering team at Transurban Consulting Engineers | none of this was a solo effort, and credit is shared with the colleagues I worked alongside on each site.
